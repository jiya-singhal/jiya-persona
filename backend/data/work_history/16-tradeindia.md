## Role
Product and Tech Intern at Tradeindia, a B2B marketplace, in Noida, from January to April 2025. This was before Sing One Song.

## Trust, safety and fraud tooling
She integrated third-party identity-verification APIs (Truecaller, IDfy and Shield) into the buyer-seller flow for fraud detection, and drove the proof-of-concept evaluation of an automated voice bot through call-transcript analysis and ASR script tuning.

## Hybrid search over messy data
She built retrieval across thousands of unstructured listings combining FAISS vector search (meaning-based match via embeddings) with FuzzyWuzzy normalisation (spelling-based match) under a weighted semantic-fuzzy-heuristic scoring layer, served through Streamlit. Each method covers the other's blind spot. Relevance went from 89 percent to 96 percent; this number is confirmed.
